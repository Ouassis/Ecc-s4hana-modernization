## Analysis
- What does the finding indicate?
- Is the finding confirmed in the target context?
- Is the object used? What evidence supports this?
- Which business processes, interfaces, or jobs depend on it?
- Is a released API or supported extension available?
- What is the proposed remediation or risk disposition?
